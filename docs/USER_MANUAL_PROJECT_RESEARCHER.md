## Welcome

You've been added to PhDapp as a **Project Researcher** for one or more students. Think of it as a *read-only seat with a shared workspace*: you can follow everything those students are doing and coordinate with their supervisors, and you get your own Drive folder and calendar that the two of you (and their supervisors) can see.

You are **not** a supervisor: you can't change a student's tasks, events or profile, and — unlike a team advisor — you never see a student's private supervisor notes or wellbeing scores.

## Signing in

1. Open the link the admin gave you and click **Sign in with Google** — pick the Gmail address you were invited with.
2. If you see "Access blocked", your address isn't on the test-users list yet — tell the admin.
3. Approve the Google consent screen (Drive + Calendar) so your shared folder and calendar work.

The browser remembers you next time.

## What a Project Researcher can do

For **each student you're assigned to**:

| You can | You cannot |
|---|---|
| See their tasks, calendar, reading, thesis & publications | Create / edit / delete their tasks or events |
| **Comment** on their tasks and events | Edit their profile or manage their team |
| Open their shared **Drive folder** (view-only) | Edit files in the student's folder |
| See their **supervision calendar** (view-only) | See private supervisor notes or wellbeing |
| Chat in the student's channels | Approve a task as done |
| Start a **Discussion** with the team | Start a *Supervisors-only* Discussion |

You only ever see the students you're assigned to — never anyone else's.

## The students you work with

You appear on the **Team** page under each student as their *Project Researcher*. The students you're assigned to are the ones whose work you can follow and whose supervisors you coordinate with. If you should be added to (or removed from) a student, ask that student's supervisor or the admin — assignment is per student, so you can work with several.

## Your workspace — folder & calendar

You get your **own** Drive folder and calendar, set up for you by a supervisor or the admin (you don't create them yourself — look for the links on your **Team** card, or ask your supervisor to set them up).

- **Drive folder** — your working folder for the project. It's shared **view-only** with the students you work with and each of their **supervisors and team advisors**. They can open and read it; only you edit it. In turn, you can open **their** shared folder view-only — so you and the student can see each other's files but neither edits the other's.
- **Calendar** — your own calendar, shared **view-only** with the same people, so your supervisors and students can see your schedule. You also see each assigned student's supervision calendar and the General calendar.

External advisors and committee members are **not** given access to your folder or calendar.

> If a folder/calendar link isn't there yet, a supervisor or admin just needs to click **Create folder / Create calendar** on your Team card. When you're later assigned to a new student, they click **Sync sharing** so the new student and their team get access.

## Tasks

Open **Tasks** to follow the board for your students (Board, List and Gantt views). You can read everything and **comment** on a task to discuss it, but you can't create, edit, move or complete tasks — that's for the student and their supervisors. Task comments are a great way to weigh in on the work.

## Calendar

The **Calendar** shows your assigned students' events, the General calendar, and your own workspace calendar. You can see 1:1 meetings, deadlines and availability. You view the students' calendars read-only; use your **own** calendar (in Google Calendar) for your project scheduling.

## Files

**Files** shows the Drive folder shared with each student you work with — click through to open in Drive (view-only for you). Your own workspace folder is linked from your **Team** card.

## Reading, thesis & publications

You can see each student's **reading list** and their **thesis chapters & publications** read-only, and follow their progress. You can't approve reading items or edit chapters — that's the supervisors' and student's job.

## Chat

You can take part in the **Chat** channels of the students you work with — a direct line to the student and their supervisors. (You won't be automatically added to a channel created before you joined; a supervisor can add you.)

## Discussions

**Discussions** are persistent topic threads for the team. You can **start a topic** (whole-team visibility) and join any discussion you can see — brainstorms, open questions, decisions. *Supervisors-only* topics are reserved for the supervising team, so you won't see or create those.

## Notifications

The 🔔 bell and the coloured dots on the sidebar tell you when something you follow changes — a new comment, a new discussion, a calendar update. Open the module to clear its dot.

## Help

Open **Help** (❓ in the sidebar) any time — it shows this manual right inside the app, with a contents list and a slide overview you can download.
